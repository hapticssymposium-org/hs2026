# Hands-on Demonstrations

Haptics Symposium strongly encourages demonstrations of all kinds - for example, of novel developments in haptics-related devices, software or experiments that are of interest to the conference audience.

Submission of Hands-on Demonstrations (Demos) is open to all members of the haptics research community. Authors of accepted papers are particularly welcome to apply for demonstration space to support their presentation. Demonstrations must be free of commercial content; companies wishing to showcase products should apply for exhibitor status.

![Smiling student volunteers welcome attendees](/img/HS2026_demos.png)

## Important Dates

* December 2025: Demo submissions open
* January 26, 2026 (11:59 p.m. AoE): Demo submission deadline
* February 12, 2026: Demo acceptance notification

## Submission Method

Proposals for Demos should be submitted using the [Google Form](https://forms.gle/wZueuxMTG67923B39).

Each submission will include the following items:

* A title that succinctly describes the hands-on demonstration.
* An ordered list of authors (including first and last names and affiliations), with an indication of who is the corresponding author.
* A 1 page PDF describing the demonstration. This should use the [ieeeconf format](http://ras.papercept.net/conferences/support/support.php).
* Any special considerations that the conference organizers should be aware of. For example, extra space requirements in the demonstration area, number of power sockets, etc.
* A video (required), see guidelines below.

Authors should submit a supplemental video that describes their demonstration, preferably with motivation for the work.

Videos will be used for promotional purposes at the conference, and all videos will be compiled into a digest video that will be posted on the conference website and uploaded to [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UC1YjMwrg8Hk6uAtyWb62yAw) to showcase current research in haptics. In consideration of this, please do not include any material in your video that you consider confidential.

When submitting a video, please follow these guidelines:

* Length: Up to 1 minute
* Size: up to 40 MB
* Format: This should be a common format (e.g. MP4). Authors should confirm that their video will play on a recent version of [VLC media player](https://www.videolan.org/vlc/index.html) (cross-platform) without the need to download additional codecs.

## Accessibility and Inclusion

We **strongly** encourage authors to prepare an accessible PDF and video for all submissions, as reviewers may have accessibility needs. We also encourage authors to use inclusive language. We encourage authors to review the [Guide to Inclusive and Accessible Submissions](../accessibility/) posted on the website prior to submission.

## Review Process

Submissions will be reviewed by the Demos and Exhibits Chairs to ensure all demonstrations are of interest to conference attendees and free from commercial content.

## Publication

The submitted title, author list, 1 page PDF and image will be included in the electronic proceedings distributed to conference attendees, but not archived on IEEExplore. As described above, all demonstration videos will be compiled into a digest video that will be posted on the conference website and uploaded to [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UC1YjMwrg8Hk6uAtyWb62yAw).

## Demonstration Details

Demonstrations will be assigned to specific sessions in which authors are required to be near their demonstration table.

We strongly recommend that demonstrators have two or more persons at their table during the session, to enable technical discussions to take place without having to pause the hands-on demonstration. If it is not possible to have more than one person present, then the demonstrator may contact the Demos and Exhibits Chairs in advance of the conference to request assistance from a student volunteer.

Demonstrators are encouraged to bring a poster to accompany their demonstration.

Each demonstration will be assigned a space at the conference. The space will include a table, chairs, electrical power, and poster board.
